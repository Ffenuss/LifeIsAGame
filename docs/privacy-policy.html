Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

The short version: Life Estate is a single-player, offline game. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There are no ads, analytics, or accounts.

1. Who we are

Life Estate is published by YOUR_DEVELOPER_NAME. This policy explains how the Android game handles information. Questions can be sent to YOUR_EMAIL_HERE.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the game does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera data
  • Advertising identifiers or device fingerprints
  • Crash reports, analytics, or usage telemetry
  • Payment or financial information

3. Data stored on your device

The game stores your current match, unlocked settings, and preferences locally in the Android WebView storage on your device. This information never leaves your device through the game. It is used only to let you continue a match and keep your settings.

You can delete this local data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the game. Android's own backup service may include app data if you have enabled device backup; that behaviour is controlled by your Android settings and Google account, not by the game.

4. Permissions

Life Estate requests only the Vibrate permission, used for optional haptic feedback. The release build does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions.

5. Third parties

The game contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social logins, or third-party trackers. No data is shared with third parties.

6. Children

Life Estate is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the game collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request deletion of.

7. Purchases and advertising

Life Estate is free, with no advertisements and no in-app purchases. It does not simulate real-money gambling or offer cash prizes.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change ships. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised, and the new policy will be published at the same URL.
